Where it all started

Bethany House Trust began with something very simple: a home opened to a child who had nowhere else to go.

In 1998, Gert and Antoinette Jonker took in a young girl who needed a safe place and full-time care. They opened their home and welcomed her into their family, not knowing that this simple, small act of kindness would grow into something much larger.

As more children came into their care, their home soon became too small.The family moved to Randfontein,but as the number of children continued to grow, they enentully moved again to Monument in Krugerdrop, where Bethany House Trust is still based today.

When started with one child became a safe place for many children who had exprienced abandoment,abuse and neglect. Bethany House became more than just a place to stay. It became a place where children could feel safe,cared for and loved

Over the years,the worl continued to grow.Bethany House Trust has helped thousands of children and young people through residential care,education,health support,youth development and community programmes

But even as the organisation has grown, the why, the reason behind it has remained the same:To give every child a safe place to belong,someone to care and hope for a better future.

Mission

We work to support children and young people facing difficult circumstances.Through our programmes and care,we help them grow,heal and build a better future,while playing our part in building a stronger community.

Vision

We want to see a future where every orphaned and vulnerable child is loved,safe,cared for and given the opportunity to learn,grow and became independent.We believe every child deserves a chance to break free from abuse and poverty and build a better life.
